Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Data we access

With the account owner's consent, the App reads: basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar), profile info, account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts) and the list of public videos with their statistics.

How we use it

Data is used only to build internal performance dashboards for accounts owned by our team. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage & retention

Access tokens and statistics are stored securely with restricted access and deleted when an account is disconnected or on request.

Your choices

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) or by contacting us.
